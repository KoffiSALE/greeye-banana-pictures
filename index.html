01 / ABOUT

Independent. African. Cinematic.

We turn real lives, ideas and histories into powerful screen stories.

Founded in Rwanda, GREEYE BANANA PICTURES works across film production, documentaries, photography and production services. We collaborate with filmmakers, broadcasters, institutions and creative partners to bring meaningful stories to audiences locally and internationally.

From development and production to post-production and delivery, we combine local knowledge with professional filmmaking practice.
